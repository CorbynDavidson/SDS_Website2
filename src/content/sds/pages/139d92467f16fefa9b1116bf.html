About Us - Manchester's Compensation Claim Specialists

Since 1997, Sheldon Davidson Solicitors has been at the forefront of securing justice and compensation for individuals across Manchester and beyond.

We have built a reputation as trusted legal specialists, helping clients with housing disrepair claims.

Personalised Legal Support

Our experienced team of solicitors combine professionalism with a personal approach. We understand that every client’s situation is unique, and we are committed to tailoring our services to your individual circumstances. Whichever type of housing disrepair you have experienced, we are here to guide you every step of the way.

Housing Disrepair Advocacy

A safe and habitable home is a fundamental right, and we work tirelessly to ensure our clients receive the repairs and compensation they’re entitled to. If you’re renting from a council or housing association and your landlord has failed to address issues like damp, mould, leaks, or structural damage, our dedicated housing disrepair team will fight for the resolution you need to restore your quality of life.

A Team You Can Trust

At Sheldon Davidson Solicitors, we take pride in the trust our clients place in us. With over 25 years of experience, we’ve honed our ability to deliver exceptional results while providing compassionate, client-focused service. Our aim is not just to win cases but to help you move forward with confidence and security.

We blend traditional legal expertise with cutting-edge technology. Our state-of-the-art case management systems ensure that your claim is handled efficiently and transparently, with regular updates to keep you informed at every stage.

Let us put our expertise to work for you. Contact us today to learn more about how we can support your claim.
