Our Focus Is Now Housing Disrepair Claims

After more than 25 years supporting clients across several areas of law, SDS Solicitors now specialises solely in housing disrepair claims. Our dedicated team helps tenants take action when landlords fail to carry out essential repairs.

SDS Solicitors Discontinued Legal Services

We understand that you may have arrived on this page because you were looking for help with a Personal Injury, Serious Injury or Medical Negligence claim. These were areas of law that SDS Solicitors proudly specialised in for more than 25 years.

As our firm has evolved, we have chosen to focus exclusively on Housing Disrepair Claims, allowing us to dedicate our expertise to helping tenants whose landlords have failed to keep their homes safe and in a proper state of repair.

Existing Personal Injury, Serious Injury and Medical Negligence Clients

If you're already a client of SDS Solicitors with an active Personal Injury, Serious Injury or Medical Negligence claim, there's no need to worry. Your case will continue to be managed by our experienced team through to its conclusion, and you can expect the same professional service and support throughout your claim.

New Personal Injury, Serious Injury and Medical Negligence Enquiries

While we continue to support our existing clients, SDS Solicitors is no longer accepting new Personal Injury, Serious Injury or Medical Negligence claims.

If you require legal advice in one of these areas, we recommend contacting a solicitor who specialises in the relevant field and is currently accepting new instructions.

Our Primary Focus is Housing Disrepair

Today, SDS Solicitors specialises exclusively in Housing Disrepair Claims, helping tenants across England, Wales and Northern Ireland hold landlords accountable when essential repairs are ignored or delayed.

We support clients with a wide range of housing disrepair and property-related claims, including:

  • Council Housing Disrepair Claims
  • Housing Association Disrepair Claims 
  • Private Landlord Disrepair Claims 
  • Damp and Mould Claims 
  • Roof Leaks and Gutter Disrepair Claims 
  • Faulty Heating and Boiler Claims
  • Electrical Hazard Claims
  • Brickwork & Structural Damage Claims
  • Pest Infestation Claims 
  • Spray Foam Insulation Claims 

If your landlord has failed to carry out repairs after being made aware of the problem, our team can assess your circumstances, explain your legal rights, and help you pursue the repairs and compensation you may be entitled to.

How SDS Solicitors Can Help

With SDS Solicitors, the solution to your home's disrepair is in safe hands. Our experienced Housing Disrepair Solicitors understand how stressful it can be when your landlord ignores or delays essential repairs. We will listen to your concerns, assess your circumstances, and explain whether you may have grounds to make a claim.

You can expect:

  • A free, no-obligation initial consultation, giving you the chance to discuss your situation and understand your options without any pressure to proceed.
  • Experienced, qualified and regulated Housing Disrepair Solicitors who understand the legal duties placed on landlords and how to move your claim forward effectively.
  • No Win, No Fee representation for eligible claims, helping you pursue repairs and compensation without taking on financial risk.
  • Clear, straightforward legal advice from start to finish, so you always understand what is happening and what the next steps are.
  • Dedicated support focused on achieving repairs and compensation as quickly as possible, helping you get your home back into the safe and proper condition it should be.
