Living in a property affected by disrepair can take a serious toll on your quality of life, health, and well-being.

Issues such as damp, mould, faulty heating, or structural defects are not only inconvenient but can also pose significant risks if left unaddressed.

If your landlord has failed to meet their legal responsibilities, you may be entitled to claim compensation and demand urgent repairs.

At SDS Solicitors, we specialise in helping tenants across England resolve housing disrepair issues.

No matter where you are, our experienced team is here to ensure your home is restored to a safe and comfortable condition.

With decades of expertise, we are dedicated to holding landlords accountable and securing the compensation you deserve under UK law.
