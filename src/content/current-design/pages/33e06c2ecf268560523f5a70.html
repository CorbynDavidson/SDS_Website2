Daisy Kerins

LL.B (Hons)

Housing Disrepair Solicitor

I am a fully qualified Housing Disrepair Solicitor and have been qualified since Summer 2026. Before qualifying as a solicitor, I worked for three years as a fee earner and paralegal, gaining valuable experience supporting clients throughout their legal journey.

I specialise in housing disrepair claims, helping tenants who are living in poor conditions secure the repairs, support, and compensation they are entitled to. I understand how stressful and upsetting these situations can be, and I pride myself on listening carefully, offering reassurance, and guiding clients with clear, practical advice.

My qualifications include:

  • First Class LLB (Bachelor of Laws) – Liverpool John Moores University
  • LPC/LLM (Legal Practice Course & Master of Laws) – Liverpool John Moores University
  • Published Author in the LJMU Student Law Journal – Criminal Evidence

Outside of work, I enjoy spending time outdoors and staying active, which helps me maintain the focus and energy to support my clients with dedication and care.
