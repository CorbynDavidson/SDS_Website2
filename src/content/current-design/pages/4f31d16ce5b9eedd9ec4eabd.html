Understanding Housing Disrepair in Bath

Housing disrepair happens when landlords, including Bath & North East Somerset Council or local housing associations, fail to keep your rented property in proper condition. They have a legal duty to maintain the structure of the home and ensure essential services and fixtures are working safely and reliably.

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Where a landlord has allowed serious disrepair to continue, tenants may be able to claim compensation alongside having the necessary repairs completed. SDS Solicitors supports clients with Housing Disrepair Claims in Bath by pursuing both outcomes, pushing for the works required to make the home safe and seeking a fair resolution for the upset, inconvenience and loss of comfort caused while the property has been in poor condition.

Compensation is usually assessed by looking at the overall impact on the home over time. This can include the rent paid, how long the defects have been present, and how significantly the disrepair has affected everyday living, for example, whether key rooms have been left damp, cold, unsafe or unusable. In general terms, the longer the problems have been ongoing and the greater the disruption, the stronger the potential claim.

SDS Solicitors reviews each case in detail, taking account of the full picture, including any effect on health and wellbeing, damage to personal belongings, and the day-to-day strain of living with unresolved defects. The aim is always to secure a fair settlement while ensuring the landlord carries out the repairs without further delay.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Bath Residents

Unresolved disrepair can affect far more than the look of a property, it can influence health, comfort and everyday wellbeing. In Bath, where many properties are older, issues such as damp, mould and water ingress can become persistent, particularly in colder and wetter weather. For households with children, older residents, or anyone with asthma or other respiratory conditions, poor internal air quality can aggravate symptoms and contribute to repeated illness.

Other defects can raise serious safety concerns. Problems such as failing plasterwork, cracked walls, broken windows, leaks from roofs or pipework, and unsafe electrics can make a home feel unsettled and, in some situations, genuinely dangerous. These are matters landlords are expected to address promptly, and a failure to do so can leave tenants living with avoidable risk.

The knock-on effects are often felt financially and emotionally. Tenants may experience damage to furniture or clothing, higher energy bills where heating is unreliable or the property is difficult to keep warm, and the ongoing inconvenience of trying to manage temporary measures while waiting for repairs. Over time, the stress of being ignored can impact sleep, mental wellbeing and the ability to feel settled at home.

No tenant should feel they have to accept these conditions. If repairs have been reported and your landlord has not taken proper action, SDS Solicitors’ Bath Housing Disrepair Solicitors can help you take the next step towards securing repairs and restoring a safe, healthy living environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim in Bath?

No, there are laws in place protecting Bath Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent in Bath if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments in Bath, throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
