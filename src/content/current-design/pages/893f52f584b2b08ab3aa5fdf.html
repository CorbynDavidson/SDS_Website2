Brian Tyrie

IT & Facilities Manager

After a successful 27-year career with the Manchester Evening News, I decided it was time for a new challenge and joined Sheldon Davidson Solicitors in 2015 as the IT and Facilities Manager.

I am responsible for overseeing the Firm's IT infrastructure, telecommunications, and day-to-day facilities management, ensuring our systems remain secure, efficient and reliable. From implementing new technology and supporting major IT projects to maintaining our office environment and coordinating with external suppliers, I play a key role in ensuring the business operates smoothly and that colleagues have the tools and support they need to deliver an excellent service to our clients.

Outside of work, I am a keen mountain biker and enjoy long hikes in the countryside, making the most of the outdoors whenever I can.
