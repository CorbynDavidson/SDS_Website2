By SheldonDavidsonSolicitors

What better way to start your day than filling your stomach with coffee and delicious cake, all whilst raising money for a fantastic cause.

This morning at Sheldon Davidson Solicitors we are in full support of the annual Macmillan Cancer Support Coffee Morning, with plenty of freshly baked delights up for grabs raising vital funds for this fantastic charity.

As a charity, Macmillan’s goal is to make sure that nobody faces cancer alone and provide medical, emotional and practical support for people with cancer.

The coffee morning is the biggest fundraising event for Macmillan with people all over the UK getting involved and hosting their own coffee mornings. Last year alone the cancer support charity raised an impressive £25 million and this year we are delighted to be involved in helping them raise even more.
