COMPENSATION WON

Ms. L, a private tenant, endured months of challenging living conditions due to ongoing disrepair in her rented property. The most serious concern involved a persistent bathroom leak, which caused severe water damage to the kitchen ceiling directly below. Despite repeatedly reporting the problem, her landlord only carried out temporary repairs such as reapplying silicone sealant, which did nothing to prevent the recurring leaks.

In addition to the bathroom issue, there were ongoing delays in repairing a broken garden fence, further adding to Ms. L’s frustration and distress over the state of her home.

Condition of the Property and Main Disrepair Issues

The most significant defect was the bathroom leak, which led to extensive damp patches and visible water damage to the kitchen ceiling and surrounding walls. The nature and persistence of the leak indicated a long-standing issue that had not been properly investigated or repaired. The lack of effective maintenance compromised both the property’s structure and the tenant’s ability to live safely and comfortably.

Impact on the Tenant’s Life

The disrepair had a serious impact on Ms. L’s wellbeing. Living with damp, leaks, and ongoing damage created daily stress and anxiety, particularly as she was caring for a young child. The situation became increasingly unsafe and unsanitary, with the tenant feeling ignored and unsupported despite her repeated attempts to seek help.

The prolonged inconvenience and the emotional strain of living in such conditions took a significant toll on Ms. L and her family’s quality of life.

How SDS Solicitors Supported the Client

After contacting SDS Solicitors, Ms. L was guided through every stage of the process by our experienced Housing Disrepair team. She was advised to gather detailed evidence, including photographs of the water damage and a full record of her communications with the landlord.

SDS Solicitors carried out a comprehensive assessment and confirmed that her claim met all legal requirements. Our team presented the evidence to the landlord in a clear and persuasive manner, ensuring her concerns were treated with the seriousness they deserved. Through effective negotiation, SDS successfully secured a fair settlement for Ms. L, reflecting the distress, disruption, and inconvenience she and her child had endured.

Outcome and Reflection

The case was resolved with a positive outcome for the tenant, ensuring accountability from the landlord and recognition of the difficulties Ms. L faced. The result highlights the importance of tenants understanding their rights and seeking professional legal support when disrepair issues are neglected.

Fee Earner’s Comment

“This case shows how even seemingly minor leaks can escalate into serious problems if ignored. By acting promptly and supporting our client throughout, we achieved a fair and just outcome that reflects the hardship she experienced.”
