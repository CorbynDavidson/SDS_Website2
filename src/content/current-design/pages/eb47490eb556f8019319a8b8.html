Understanding Housing Disrepair in Coventry

Housing disrepair refers to a failure by landlords, whether Coventry City Council or housing association, to keep a rented property in good repair. This includes maintaining the structure and systems essential for a habitable home, such as heating, plumbing, and electrical safety. Common disrepair issues tenants face include:

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Compensation for a housing disrepair claim is usually assessed by looking at the overall effect the problems have had on the household, including how serious the defects are, how long they have continued, and the extent to which they have disrupted normal living. Where key rooms or facilities have been affected, such as a bathroom that cannot be used properly or a kitchen that is significantly restricted, a claim can include an element to reflect that loss of use and enjoyment of the home.

In many cases, compensation is calculated by applying a rent-based percentage for the period impacted. A common bracket is around 25% to 50% of annual rent, although more severe or long-running situations may justify a higher figure. SDS Solicitors will assess the full circumstances carefully, ensuring all relevant losses are considered, including damaged belongings, additional expenses, inconvenience, and any impact on health and wellbeing.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Coventry Residents

In Coventry, disrepair that is left unresolved can affect far more than the condition of the building, it can have a real impact on health, wellbeing and finances. Damp and mould are common problems in older properties and, when allowed to persist, can aggravate asthma and other breathing difficulties, contribute to allergies, and leave households feeling unwell over time. Where the issues involve structural defects or safety-related faults, the home may also become difficult, and sometimes unsafe, to live in.

The knock-on effects are often practical and costly. Tenants may face damaged belongings, higher energy bills where heating systems are inefficient or unreliable, and the expense of temporary measures while waiting for the landlord to act. Many people also experience emotional strain, including stress and a reduced sense of comfort in their own home.

Tenants do not have to deal with these conditions alone. If a landlord is not addressing repairs properly, taking legal advice can help clarify what action can be taken to secure repairs and improve living conditions.

What authorities govern housing disrepair in Coventry?

Social housing in England can be handled by either a Council, which is a government authority, or by private Housing Associations which rent out properties. In Coventry, and in the wider West Midlands area, your local Council is likely to be Coventry City Council however other authorities may operate in the surrounding areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim in Coventry?

No, there are laws in place protecting Coventry Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a Coventry disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
