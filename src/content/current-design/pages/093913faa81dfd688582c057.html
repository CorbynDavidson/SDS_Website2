Sheldon Davidson

LL.B (Hons)

Managing Director

Sheldon is the Managing Director of Sheldon Davidson Solicitors Limited and is a Personal Injury solicitor of 27 years’ experience.

Sheldon studied law in Manchester and before going on to qualify as a solicitor had several other commercial interests. However, always with a passion for law, Sheldon went on to study at Chester Law School and qualified as a Solicitor in 1994.

Having initially worked in general practice for 5 years at a firm in Macclesfield, Sheldon established 'Sheldon Davidson Solicitors' in 1997 with a staff of two to support him, the firm now employs over 40 full and part-time employees, hand-picked for their professional skills. Sheldon takes great pride from watching the firm grow to what it is today and attributes the firm's success to the quality of service for which it has become known, as well as for its personal and professional approach combined with strong ties to the community. Sheldon feels the greatest compliment he can receive is when someone says "you are not like other solicitors" due to a personable, caring and approachable style of client service which pervades the entire firm.

Sheldon remains committed to the firm and its future success, championing local strength, its independence and real care for the clients we all work with. Sheldon’s mission remains as strong as ever, to provide a quality service with a professional yet personable approach that sets the firm apart from its larger national competitors
