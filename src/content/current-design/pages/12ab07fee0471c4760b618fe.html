Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Compensation in housing disrepair claims is assessed on the facts of each case, including how serious the defects are, how long they have persisted, and the extent to which they have affected everyday living. If certain rooms or essential facilities have not been usable, for example, a shower, bathroom, kitchen appliance or heating, compensation may be calculated as a proportion of the rent for the period those areas were effectively out of use.

In many cases, compensation is commonly valued at around 25% to 50% of the annual rent for the affected timeframe, although particularly severe or prolonged disrepair can justify a higher figure. The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Huddersfield Residents

Living with unresolved disrepair can create serious strain, physically, emotionally and financially. In Huddersfield, tenants may find that issues such as damp and mould are more common in older properties or homes affected by poor ventilation and persistent moisture. These conditions can aggravate respiratory problems, trigger allergies and, over time, contribute to longer-term health concerns, particularly for children, older residents and those with existing medical conditions. Where a property is also affected by structural defects, the risks can increase further, making parts of the home unsafe or unsuitable for everyday living.

The consequences are often felt in the household budget as well. Disrepair can lead to damage to belongings, higher energy costs where heating is inefficient, and the expense of temporary measures when repairs are delayed. Many tenants also experience a real impact on mental wellbeing, including stress, embarrassment, disrupted sleep and a reduced ability to feel comfortable at home.

If a landlord is not taking appropriate action, tenants do not have to cope alone. Getting early legal advice can be an important step towards securing repairs and improving living conditions.

What authorities govern housing disrepair in Huddersfield?

Social housing in England can be handled by either a Council, which is a government authority, or by private Housing Associations which rent out properties. In Huddersfield, and in the wider West Yorkshire area, your local Council is likely to be Kirklees Council however other authorities may operate in the surrounding areas.

If your property is under the responsibility of a private Housing Association, this organisation must be properly registered and approved by the UK Government to operate in England. You can check the details of your association on the list of registered providers on the Government’s website. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Huddersfield Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a Huddersfield disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
