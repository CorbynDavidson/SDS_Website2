Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

The amount of compensation you may receive for ​​​​​​ housing disrepair claims depends on the severity of the issue, how long it has been ongoing, and the effect it has had on your daily life. If key areas of your home, such as your kitchen or bathroom, have been unusable due to disrepair, your compensation is usually calculated as a percentage of your rent.

In most cases, tenants can expect to recover between 25% and 50% of their annual rent for the period affected, though more serious cases may result in higher compensation. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Leicester Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a Leicester disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.

Can I claim if I get housing benefit or universal credit?

Yes. Receiving Housing Benefit or Universal Credit does not usually prevent you from making a housing disrepair claim against your Leicester landlord. If your landlord has failed to carry out necessary repairs after being notified, SDS Solicitors can assess your case and advise whether you may be entitled to repairs and compensation.

Can I claim if my tenancy has ended?

Yes. You may still be able to make a housing disrepair claim after your Leicester tenancy has ended, provided the problems occurred while you were living in the property and your former landlord had an opportunity to deal with them. SDS Solicitors can review your former tenancy, the disrepair history, and any evidence you have to advise whether a claim can still be pursued.

What should I do if the landlord says they never received my complaint?

If your Leicester landlord says they never received your complaint, any emails, text messages, repair reference numbers, photographs, or records of phone calls can help show when the disrepair was reported. SDS Solicitors can review the evidence available and advise on the best way to establish that your landlord was made aware of the problem.
