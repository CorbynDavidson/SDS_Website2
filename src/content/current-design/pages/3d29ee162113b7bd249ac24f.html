Understanding Housing Disrepair in Sheffield

Housing disrepair occurs when a landlord, whether Sheffield City Council or a housing association, fails to keep a rented property in a safe and liveable condition. This includes maintaining the structure of the building and ensuring that essential systems such as heating, plumbing, and electrical wiring are in proper working order. Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

The amount of compensation you can receive for housing disrepair claims depends on the severity of the problem, how long it has persisted, and the impact it has had on your daily life. If essential parts of your home, such as your bathroom or kitchen, have been unusable due to disrepair, your compensation is typically calculated as a percentage of your rent.

In most cases, tenants can expect to recover between 25% and 50% of their annual rent for the period affected, with more serious cases sometimes exceeding this range. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.

Can I claim if the problem was present when I moved in?

Yes. If the disrepair was already present when you moved into your Sheffield home, you may still be able to claim if your landlord knew, or should reasonably have known, about the problem and failed to put it right. SDS Solicitors can review when the issue began, what your landlord was told, and whether you have grounds to pursue repairs and compensation.

How long should I give the landlord to carry out repairs?

There is no single fixed timeframe, as your Sheffield landlord must carry out repairs within a reasonable period depending on how serious and urgent the problem is. If you have reported the disrepair and your landlord has delayed or failed to act, SDS Solicitors can assess whether the response has been unreasonable and advise you on making a housing disrepair claim.

Will the claim settle without a hearing?

Many Sheffield housing disrepair claims are resolved without the need for a court hearing, particularly where the landlord agrees to carry out the necessary repairs and negotiate compensation. SDS Solicitors will aim to resolve your claim as efficiently as possible, but if court proceedings become necessary, we can guide and represent you throughout the process.
