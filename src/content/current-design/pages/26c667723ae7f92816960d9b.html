Understanding Housing Disrepair in Lancaster

Housing disrepair happens when landlords, including Lancaster City Council or local housing associations, fail to keep your rented property in proper condition. They have a legal duty to maintain the structure of the home and ensure essential services and fixtures are working safely and reliably.

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Where serious disrepair has affected your living conditions, you may be able to claim compensation alongside requiring the landlord to complete the necessary repairs. SDS Solicitors supports tenants with Lancaster Housing Disrepair Claims, working to recover a fair settlement that reflects the disruption caused and to ensure the property is restored to a safe, habitable standard.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Lancaster Residents

In Lancaster, poor housing conditions can affect far more than comfort, they can have lasting consequences for health, wellbeing and day-to-day living. Older properties can be more vulnerable to damp and mould, particularly during colder periods when homes are harder to heat and ventilate. These issues may aggravate asthma and other respiratory conditions, contribute to chest infections, and be especially concerning for children, older residents and anyone with existing medical needs.

Some forms of disrepair also raise safety concerns. Water ingress, broken windows, unstable plasterwork, cracked walls or unsafe electrics can make parts of a home hazardous and may indicate that a landlord has not met their repair obligations.

The financial impact can be significant as well. Tenants may have to replace belongings damaged by damp, face higher energy bills where heating systems are inefficient or unreliable, or spend money on temporary measures while waiting for repairs. Many households also experience emotional strain, including stress, disrupted sleep and a reduced sense of security at home.

Where repairs are not being dealt with properly, tenants do not have to manage it alone. SDS Solicitors’ Lancaster Housing Disrepair Solicitors can advise on the options for securing repairs and pursuing a fair outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim in Lancaster?

No, there are laws in place protecting Lancaster Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a Lancaster disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
