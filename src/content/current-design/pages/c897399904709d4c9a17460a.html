Emma Farrington

Fee Earner

I have over 20 years of experience in the legal industry, having progressed from Legal Secretary through to a Fee Earner managing my own caseload, with particular expertise in personal injury and road traffic accident (RTA) claims.

My experience includes handling claims valued up to £25,000, dealing with a wide variety of losses such as loss of earnings, care and assistance and general damages for injury. I am highly experienced in guiding clients through what can often be a daunting process, ensuring their claims are advanced thoroughly and with care.

Over the years, I have built a reputation for being detail-driven, approachable, and client-focused, with a strong ability to manage cases efficiently while providing clear communication and support at every stage. My progression through different roles in the profession has given me a deep understanding not only of case handling but also of the practical and procedural aspects that underpin successful outcomes.

Outside of work, I am passionate about maintaining an active lifestyle through the gym, exploring new destinations through travel, and enjoying good food with family and friends.
