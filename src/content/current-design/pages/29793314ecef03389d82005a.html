Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

If your home has been affected by serious disrepair, you may be entitled to recover financial compensation in addition to securing the repairs needed to put the property back into proper condition. SDS Solicitors handles Shrewsbury housing disrepair claims, working to ensure tenants receive a fair outcome that reflects the disruption and discomfort caused while the problems remained unresolved.

Compensation is often assessed by considering the overall impact of the disrepair, including how long the issues have continued, how severe they are, and how much of the home has been affected. A common approach is to look at the monthly rent across the period impacted and apply a percentage to reflect the level of interference with normal use of the property. Generally, the longer the disrepair has persisted and the more areas of the home that have been affected, the stronger the potential value of the claim may be.

At SDS Solicitors, every claim is reviewed carefully to ensure no eligible element is missed. This includes the effect on health and wellbeing, damage to belongings, and the stress, inconvenience or additional costs experienced. The aim is to secure appropriate compensation while ensuring the landlord completes the required repairs without further delay.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Shrewsbury Residents

Living in a home with ongoing disrepair can affect both physical health and emotional wellbeing, particularly when problems are left unresolved for long periods. In Shrewsbury, tenants in older properties may be more likely to experience issues such as damp and mould, which can worsen during colder months where ventilation is reduced and condensation increases. These conditions can contribute to breathing difficulties, recurring chest infections and longer-term health concerns, especially for children, older residents and anyone with pre-existing medical issues.

Disrepair can also present serious safety concerns. Structural defects and poorly maintained installations, such as leaking roofs, broken windows, cracks to walls or exposed electrics, can leave a property feeling unsafe and can put residents at risk. These are not minor faults; they may indicate a landlord has failed to meet their legal responsibilities.

The impact is often financial as well. Tenants may face damage to personal belongings, higher heating bills where systems are inefficient or unreliable, and the cost of temporary measures when landlords delay repairs. Over time, the disruption can also take an emotional toll, with many residents experiencing anxiety, poor sleep and a reduced ability to feel comfortable at home. When a property is not properly maintained, even everyday routines can become difficult.

No tenant should have to tolerate these conditions for a long period. If your landlord has failed to act, speaking to SDS Solicitors’ Shrewsbury Housing Disrepair Solicitors can be an important first step towards securing repairs and restoring your right to a safe and healthy home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
