Sheldon Davidson Solicitors offers a housing disrepair claims service with a difference

A firm of housing disrepair solicitors in Manchester is standing out from the crowd by providing a personal and dedicated service, reflected in its long-standing belief in being “Proud to be Independent”.

Sheldon Davidson Solicitors has been established in Greater Manchester for many years, building a strong reputation for approachable legal advice and determined representation. By combining local knowledge with a personal and caring service, the firm remains distinct from large, impersonal national claims businesses.

Today, Sheldon Davidson Solicitors specialises in housing disrepair claims for tenants living in council properties, housing association homes, and accommodation managed by charitable organisations. The team supports tenants whose landlords have failed to deal with problems such as damp, mould, leaks, defective heating, damaged windows, structural defects, electrical hazards, pest infestations, and other serious repair issues.

The solicitors and support staff strongly believe that every client should be treated as an individual. Living in an unsafe or poorly maintained home can be deeply distressing. It may affect a tenant’s health, family life, finances, and sense of security. For that reason, the team takes time to understand each client’s circumstances and the condition of their home before advising them on the best way forward.

From the beginning of a claim, clients receive clear guidance about their rights, the evidence they may need, and the steps available to secure repairs and compensation. The team understands that many tenants have already reported problems repeatedly without meaningful action from their landlord. Sheldon Davidson Solicitors works to ensure those concerns are taken seriously.

This compassionate and independent approach runs throughout the firm’s work:

  • Initial advice is always no obligation and free of charge
  • Callers speak to a qualified legal professional in our Manchester offices, not an outsourced call centre worker
  • Phone lines are open Monday to Friday 9 am-10 pm, 9 am-5 pm on Saturdays and 10 am-4 pm on Sundays, for the convenience of claimants
  • Solicitors can be emailed directly from the website, or seen with no appointments necessary at the office in Whitefield
  • Claims are always settled out of court where possible, securing the maximum possible amount of compensation for clients
  • A first name culture and a guarantee to see cases through from beginning to end gives the business a human face

It’s plain to see why Founder and Managing Director Sheldon Davidson takes great pride in being told, “you’re not like other solicitors”.
