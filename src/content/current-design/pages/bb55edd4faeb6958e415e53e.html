Victoria McCormack

Managing Partner

I’ve been with the firm for over 26 years, having started in an entry-level role as the Office Junior and worked my way up through a variety of positions. This experience has given me a comprehensive understanding of the firm’s operations and the demands of legal practice at every level.

As Practice Manager, I oversee the daily operations of the firm, ensuring that everything runs smoothly behind the scenes so that our legal teams can focus on delivering excellent service to our clients. My role spans a wide range of responsibilities, including compliance, staffing, client care, financial processes, supporting strategic development and operational support.

Over the years, I’ve gained considerable experience in managing administrative and procedural requirements, and I remain focused on ensuring that high standards are consistently met across the firm.

In addition to my managerial responsibilities, I continue to maintain a direct involvement in fee-earning work. This allows me to stay actively engaged in casework, which is not only personally rewarding but also enables me to better support our fee earners in their roles. By continuing to work on live files, I remain up to date with legal and procedural developments, and I’m able to share practical, real-time insights with the wider team. This helps in guiding newer colleagues, streamlining working practices, and embedding continuous learning across the firm.

I’m proud to have grown with the firm and to be part of a team that shares a commitment to professionalism, integrity, and care in everything we do.

Outside of work, I enjoy Pilates, reading, and spending time on holiday with my family.
