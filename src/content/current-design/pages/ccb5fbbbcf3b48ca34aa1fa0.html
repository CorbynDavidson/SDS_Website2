£2,750 Compensation for Tenant After Roof Leak and Widespread Damp

COMPENSATION WON

£2,750

Ms. T was a tenant living in a rented property in Bicester. From the outset of her tenancy, she experienced ongoing issues linked to the roof, with repeated leaks causing increasing concern about dampness spreading throughout the home. As time went on, Ms. T became more worried that moisture was seeping into the walls and potentially affecting the wider structure.

Condition of the Property and Main Disrepair Issues

The main cause of the disrepair was a faulty roof, with missing tiles allowing water to enter the loft space and seep into the ceilings below. Over time, this led to widespread signs of water penetration and dampness across multiple rooms. The living room ceiling showed cracking and defects. In the kitchen, there were ceiling defects with visible water staining, a strong smell of damp, and a faulty extractor fan. The bathroom ceiling was also defective with water staining present. Damp was additionally reported on the bedroom walls, including areas above and around a window.

Ms. T reported being able to smell mould and damp throughout the property and was particularly concerned that moisture was spreading into the walls and structural framework.

Impact on the Tenant’s Life

The continued disrepair had a noticeable impact on Ms. T’s day-to-day life. Living with recurring leaks, damp smells, and visible staining in key areas of the home was distressing and disruptive. The ongoing nature of the problem—particularly where water ingress appeared to be spreading—caused anxiety and left Ms. T feeling unsettled within her own living space.

How SDS Solicitors Supported the Client

SDS Solicitors progressed the matter using an evidence-led approach, ensuring the landlord’s repairing responsibilities were properly addressed. The team:

  • Took detailed instructions on the history of the leaks and how long the issues had persisted
  • Collated evidence of the disrepair across each affected area of the property
  • Pressed for action to resolve the root cause of the problem (the defective roof), rather than relying on repeated internal patch repairs
  • Negotiated firmly to ensure the client’s concerns were treated seriously and resolved within a reasonable timeframe

Throughout, SDS Solicitors maintained clear communication and ensured Ms. T felt supported while the matter progressed.

Outcome and Reflection

The claim concluded successfully. The disrepair was progressed and the matter resolved with £2,750 recovered in compensation to reflect the disruption and distress Ms. T experienced as a result of the prolonged roof leak, dampness, and associated defects within the home.

This case demonstrates how roof defects can quickly escalate into widespread internal disrepair and why tenants should not be left to tolerate poor living conditions when repairs are overdue. SDS Solicitors ensured the client’s case was handled sensitively and robustly, resulting in a fair and practical outcome.

Fee Earner’s Comment

“Where roof leaks persist, the impact on a tenant’s home can be extensive and extremely distressing. This case required persistence to ensure the underlying cause was addressed properly. We are pleased to have secured compensation for the client and a positive resolution to the disrepair.”
