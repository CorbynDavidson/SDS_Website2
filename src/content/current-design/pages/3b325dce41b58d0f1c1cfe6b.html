Understanding Housing Disrepair in Manchester

Housing disrepair refers to a failure by landlords, whether Manchester City Council or housing association, to keep a rented property in good repair. This includes maintaining the structure and systems essential for a habitable home, such as heating, plumbing, and electrical safety. Common disrepair issues tenants face include:

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

If your landlord has failed to maintain your home and you have been left living in poor or unsafe conditions, you may be entitled to compensation. The amount you can claim depends on how serious the disrepair is, how long it has been left unresolved, and how much it has affected your daily life.

For example, if your kitchen or bathroom has not been usable for a long period, you could receive compensation that reflects the loss of those essential facilities. In most cases, tenants receive between 25 and 50 percent of their annual rent for the time their home was in disrepair. In more severe cases, the figure can be even higher.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.

How do I know if I can make a housing disrepair claim?

If you have reported repair issues to your landlord and they have failed to take action within a reasonable time, you may be eligible to make a housing disrepair claim. Common problems include damp and mould, faulty electrics, broken heating, leaks, and structural damage. Our Housing Disrepair Solicitors in Manchester can assess your case and advise you on your next steps, all at no cost to you.

Can I claim compensation if I am still living in the property?

Yes, absolutely. You do not need to move out to make a claim. Most of our Manchester housing disrepair clients continue living in their homes while we work to get the repairs done and compensation paid. You can claim for the inconvenience, damage to your belongings, increased living costs and any impact on your health or well-being.
