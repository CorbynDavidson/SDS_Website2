Housing Disrepair Claims Newham

Living in a home affected by disrepair can have a serious impact on your health, comfort, and daily routine. Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Compensation for housing disrepair depends on the seriousness of the problem, how long it has continued, and how it has affected your daily life. If essential parts of your home such as your kitchen or bathroom have not been usable, you may be entitled to a percentage of your rent for the time those areas were out of use.

In many cases, tenants can expect to receive between 25% and 50% of their annual rent as compensation. In more severe situations, this amount may be higher. The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Newham Residents

Living with unresolved disrepair can affect your health, your finances, and your ability to feel comfortable in your home. For tenants in Newham, damp and mould may develop where there are leaks, poor ventilation, defective windows, cold walls, or heating problems. These conditions can make the property feel unhealthy and may worsen asthma, breathing difficulties, allergies, or other existing medical concerns.

Serious defects can also make a home unsafe. Leaking roofs, cracked walls, damaged ceilings, unstable flooring, faulty wiring, or broken windows can interfere with daily life and may put you and your family at risk. If these issues are not repaired, they can deteriorate further and make the property increasingly difficult to live in.

Disrepair can also cause financial loss. Damp, mould, and leaks may damage furniture, bedding, clothing, carpets, and other personal belongings. Poor heating or insulation can make energy bills higher, while repeated delays from your landlord can lead to stress, frustration, embarrassment, and a loss of enjoyment of your home.

If your landlord has been made aware of the disrepair and has failed to take proper action, you do not have to continue living in poor conditions. What authorities govern housing disrepair in Newham?

Social housing in England can be handled by either a Council, which is a government authority, or by private Housing Associations which rent out properties. In Newham, your local Council is likely to be Newham Borough Council however other authorities may operate in the surrounding areas.

If your property is under the responsibility of a private Housing Association, this organisation must be properly registered and approved by the UK Government to operate in England. You can check the details of your association on the list of registered providers on the Government’s website. In Newham, some of the most common housing associations include:

  • ASRA Greater London Housing Association
  • Major Housing Association
  • Home From Home Housing Assocation
  • Local Space
  • The Guinness South
  • L & Q
  • Livingstone Health
  • Longlife Housing Co-Operative
  • London Borough of Newham

Other private providers operate in Newham, and you can still make a claim for disrepair issues if you are dealing with a provider who does not have a local office in Newham.

  • Have you taken photographs, videos, and notes detailing the problems?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Newham Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a Newham disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
