Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

The amount of compensation you can claim for housing disrepair depends on factors such as the severity, duration, and impact of the problem on your daily life. If essential areas of your home, such as your kitchen, bathroom, or heating system, have been out of use, you could be entitled to compensation based on a proportion of your rent. For example, if you’ve been unable to use your shower or cooker for an extended period, this could significantly affect the amount you receive.

In most cases, compensation falls between 25% and 50% of your annual rent for the time you have been affected. However, in severe cases, the amount could be even higher. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.

What should I do if a vulnerable person lives in the property?

If a vulnerable person lives in the property and your landlord has failed to resolve the disrepair after being notified, SDS Solicitors can help you take further action. We can assess your circumstances, pursue the repairs your household needs, and advise whether you may also be entitled to compensation for your landlord’s failure to act.

What evidence do I need to make a Leeds housing disrepair claim?

Useful evidence can include photographs or videos of the disrepair, copies of messages or emails sent to your landlord, repair records, and any medical evidence where your health has been affected. SDS Solicitors can review the evidence you already have and advise whether anything further is needed to support your Leeds housing disrepair claim.

What counts as an emergency repair in Leeds?

An emergency repair is usually a problem that creates an immediate risk to health, safety, or the property, such as a serious water leak, dangerous electrical fault, loss of essential services, or major structural damage. If your Leeds landlord has failed to deal with an urgent repair after being notified, SDS Solicitors can advise you on your rights and whether you may be able to make a housing disrepair claim.
