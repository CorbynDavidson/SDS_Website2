COMPENSATION WON

Mr. J and his young family moved into their privately rented home in October 2022, looking forward to a fresh start. Unfortunately, soon after moving in, they began to encounter serious issues with the property’s condition. What should have been a safe and comfortable home quickly became a source of ongoing distress and concern.

Condition of the Property and Main Disrepair Issues

Significant gaps and holes developed in the flooring throughout the property, creating trip hazards and making the home unsafe to navigate—particularly for the family’s young child. In addition, a persistent mouse infestation emerged, further compromising the family’s living conditions. Despite Mr. J promptly reporting the issues, the landlord failed to act and instead placed responsibility for the infestation on the tenants themselves.

The combination of hazardous flooring and a pest problem presented both health and safety risks. The open gaps in the floor not only caused physical danger but also allowed pests to enter the property more easily, worsening the infestation.

Impact on the Tenant’s Life

The disrepair had a profound effect on the family’s wellbeing. Living among pests and unsafe flooring caused constant stress and anxiety, making daily life difficult. Mr. J was particularly concerned for his child’s safety, fearing accidents or health complications. What should have been a family home became a daily reminder of neglect and disrepair.

How SDS Solicitors Supported the Client

Upon instructing SDS Solicitors, Mr. J received immediate, professional support. The team carried out a detailed inspection, obtained photographic evidence, and documented the extent of the disrepair. SDS engaged directly with the landlord, asserting the tenant’s rights and pressing for urgent remedial action. Through effective negotiation, SDS Solicitors achieved a fair settlement that acknowledged the disruption, inconvenience, and distress suffered by the family.

Outcome and Reflection

This case was successfully resolved with a fair settlement and the landlord’s agreement to address the disrepair. Mr. J and his family can now look forward to a safer, healthier living environment.

This matter underscores the importance of tenants knowing their rights when faced with disrepair and highlights SDS Solicitors’ commitment to ensuring landlords fulfil their legal obligations.

Fee Earner’s Comment

“This case required persistence and a strong focus on the family’s safety and wellbeing. We are pleased to have achieved a positive resolution that brings our client peace of mind and a safer home.”
