Understanding Housing Disrepair in Colchester

Housing disrepair refers to a failure by landlords, whether Colchester City Council or housing association, to keep a rented property in good repair. Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Compensation in a housing disrepair claim is usually worked out by looking at the overall impact of the problems, including how serious they are, how long they have been left unresolved, and the extent to which they have disrupted normal living. Where key facilities have been affected, such as a bathroom that cannot be used properly or a kitchen that is significantly restricted, the claim can include an element to reflect the reduced use and enjoyment of the home.

In practice, awards are often assessed by applying a rent-based percentage for the period affected. A common bracket is around 25% to 50% of annual rent, although particularly severe or long-running disrepair can justify a higher figure. SDS Solicitors will review the full circumstances carefully, ensuring the claim also accounts for related losses such as damaged belongings, additional expenses, inconvenience, and any effect on health and wellbeing.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Colchester Residents

In Colchester, unresolved disrepair can have a real impact on health, comfort and finances, particularly in older properties where damp and mould can be an ongoing issue. Persistent moisture problems may worsen asthma and other respiratory conditions, contribute to allergies, and leave households feeling unwell over time. Where there are structural defects or safety-related faults, the home can also become difficult, and sometimes unsafe, to live in.

The consequences are often felt beyond physical health. Tenants may face the cost of replacing damaged belongings, higher energy bills where heating systems are inefficient or unreliable, and the expense of temporary measures while waiting for repairs. Many people also experience emotional strain, including stress, embarrassment and a reduced sense of wellbeing when living conditions fall below a reasonable standard.

Tenants do not have to cope alone. If a landlord has failed to deal with disrepair properly, obtaining legal advice can help clarify what steps can be taken to secure repairs and improve living conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim in Colchester?

No, there are laws in place protecting Colchester Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a Colchester disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
