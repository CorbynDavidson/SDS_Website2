Angela Brewis

Cashier

I joined Sheldon Davidson Solicitors in January 2000 as the firm’s most enthusiastic 37-year-old “office junior” after taking a 10-year career break to raise my children. Stepping back into office life after a long pause was a big move, but I was welcomed with open arms and quickly felt part of the team.

Since then, I’ve had the opportunity to grow and develop within the firm, progressing through various roles and gaining valuable experience along the way. Now, over two decades later, I’m proud to be part of the accounts team, where I continue to contribute to the day-to-day running of the practice.

Outside of work, I love spending quality time with my family, especially my grandchildren, who keep me busy and bring me so much joy.
