Understanding Housing Disrepair in London

Housing disrepair refers to a failure by landlords, whether Greater London Authority (GLA) or housing association, to keep a rented property in good repair. This includes maintaining the structure and systems essential for a habitable home, such as heating, plumbing, and electrical safety. Common disrepair issues tenants face include:

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Tenants affected by serious disrepair may be entitled to financial compensation in addition to having the underlying issues resolved. Our Housing Disrepair Solicitors in London work to secure both, helping clients recover compensation for the disruption and distress caused.

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims in London depends on how long the problem has existed, how severe it is, and how it has affected your ability to use and enjoy your home. If key areas such as your bathroom, kitchen or heating have been unusable, you could receive a percentage of your rent for the period you were affected.

In most cases, compensation falls between 25 percent and 50 percent of your annual rent. In more severe cases, where the disrepair has caused serious inconvenience or health problems, this amount can be higher.

We will also consider any wider impact the disrepair has had. This includes physical health, emotional well-being and financial losses. At SDS Solicitors, we make sure every aspect of your situation is properly assessed to maximise your claim.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on London Residents

Housing disrepair is more than an inconvenience. For many tenants in the capital, it causes serious physical, emotional and financial strain. In London, damp and mould are especially common due to the city’s climate and the age of much of the housing stock. These conditions can lead to respiratory problems, skin irritations and other long-term health issues.

Structural defects and faulty installations can make homes unsafe to live in. Broken windows, cracked walls, faulty wiring or leaking roofs are not just breaches of your landlord’s duty, they are hazards that put your health and safety at risk.

Disrepair can also have a significant financial impact. Tenants often face damage to personal belongings, increased heating costs from inefficient systems, and the added burden of temporary fixes. Emotionally, many residents report anxiety, loss of sleep and a general decline in their mental well-being. The stress of living in unsafe or unfit housing can make everyday life incredibly difficult.

What Authorities Govern Housing Disrepair in London?

Social housing in England can be handled by either a Council, which is a government authority, or by private Housing Associations which rent out properties. In London and in the wider area, your local Council is likely to be Greater London Authority (GLA) however other authorities may operate in the surrounding areas.

If your property is under the responsibility of a private Housing Association, this organisation must be properly registered and approved by the UK Government to operate in England. You can check the details of your association on the list of registered providers on the Government’s website. In London and Greater London, some of the most common housing associations include:

  • Seven Dials Housing
  • English Rural Housing Association
  • Habinteg Housing Association
  • Soho Housing
  • Teachers Housing Association
  • Look Ahead Housing & Care
  • Wandle Housing Association
  • Ekaya Housing Association
  • ASRA Greater London Housing Association
  • Focus Project-Penrose Housing Association

Other private providers operate in London, and you can still make a claim if you are dealing with a provider who does not have a local office in London.

Am I Eligible for a Housing Disrepair Claim in London?

If you believe you have grounds for a housing disrepair claim for a London home, you must be able to answer the following questions:

  • Have you reported the disrepair more than 6 months ago?
  • Is this a permanent property in London?
  • Is responsibility for the disrepair held by a council or housing association?
  • Have you made a Housing Disrepair Claim in London within the last 12 months?
  • Have you taken photographs, videos, and notes documenting the problems?
  • Have you gathered up-to-date records of any health issues or financial losses caused by the disrepair?
  • Are you in rental arrears? If so, do you have a payment plan in place?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.

Can I make a claim against a housing association in London?

Yes, our London Housing Disrepair Solicitors regularly act on behalf of tenants in both council and housing association homes where landlords have failed to carry out necessary repairs.

Can I claim for housing disrepair if my landlord is a London Borough Council?

Yes. Whether you rent from Southwark, Hackney, Camden, or any other London borough, your council has a legal duty to keep the property in good repair. If they fail, our London Housing Disrepair Solicitors can help you claim.

Is overcrowding linked to Housing Disrepair in London?

Yes, overcrowding is common in many older London estates and often makes issues like damp and mould much worse. These kinds of problems can be included in London Housing Disrepair Claims, especially when they’ve been reported but ignored by your landlord.

Can I still claim if my home is part of a regeneration scheme in London?

Yes, being part of a regeneration scheme doesn’t remove your landlord’s responsibility to maintain safe living conditions.

Even if your estate is due to be redeveloped, serious repair issues shouldn’t be ignored. These situations are often the basis for a lot of London Housing Disrepair Claims

Are housing standards different in inner vs outer London Boroughs?

No—whether you live in Camden or Croydon, all social landlords must meet national repair standards.

Our London Housing Disrepair Solicitors cover all boroughs and will ensure your rights are protected.

Can I claim for disrepair in a London tower block or estate flat?

Absolutely, many London Disrepair Claims come from residents in high-rise council blocks or older estate housing where issues like leaks, damp, and heating failures are common.
