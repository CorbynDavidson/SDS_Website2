Michael Haycock

LL.B (Hons)

Solicitor

With over 30 years of experience in personal injury law, I have built a strong reputation for securing justice and fair compensation for clients from all walks of life. My practice spans the full range of accident claims, including road traffic and cycling accidents, workplace incidents, slips and falls, aviation accidents, and Criminal Injuries Compensation Authority (CICA) claims.

I specialise in representing seriously and catastrophically injured Claimants, including those who have sustained traumatic brain injuries, amputations, and severe psychiatric injuries. Many of the cases I handle are of significant value, with settlements reaching up to £2.5 million.

Beyond securing financial compensation, I am passionate about ensuring my clients get access to the right rehabilitation and support packages at the earliest opportunity. I work closely with medical and rehabilitation experts to arrange tailored, comprehensive programmes that help rebuild lives after serious injury.

Clients value my approach as both professional and personable. I believe in providing clear, practical advice while being approachable and supportive throughout what is often an overwhelming process.

Outside of work, I enjoy keeping active at the gym, mountain biking, following motorsport, and — when time allows — unwinding with reality TV.
