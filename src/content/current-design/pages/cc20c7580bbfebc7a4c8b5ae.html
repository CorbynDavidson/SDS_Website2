Housing disrepair happens when landlords, including Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council or local housing associations, fail to keep your rented property in proper condition. They have a legal duty to maintain the structure of the home and ensure essential services and fixtures are working safely and reliably.

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

If a landlord has allowed serious disrepair to continue, tenants may have the right to seek compensation as well as insisting that repairs are completed. SDS Solicitors supports clients with Housing Disrepair Claims in Barnsley by pursuing the works needed to make the property safe and by seeking a fair outcome for the distress, inconvenience and loss of enjoyment caused while the home has been in poor condition.

Compensation is often assessed by considering the level of disruption to the property over time. Factors can include the rent paid, how long the problems have persisted, and how much of the home has been affected, for example, whether key rooms such as bedrooms, kitchens or bathrooms have been impacted. Generally, the more severe the disrepair and the longer it has gone on, the stronger the claim is likely to be.

SDS Solicitors takes a detailed approach to valuing each case. The team will consider the practical and personal impact, including any effect on health and wellbeing, damage to belongings, and the day-to-day stress caused by living with unresolved defects. The aim is always to secure a fair settlement while ensuring the landlord carries out the necessary repairs without further delay.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Barnsley Residents

Housing disrepair is not just an inconvenience, it can affect day-to-day comfort, health and a family’s sense of security at home. In Barnsley, tenants living in properties with ongoing damp and mould often find the problems worsen in colder, wetter periods, making rooms feel unusable and difficult to keep warm. For some households, particularly those with children, older residents, or existing respiratory conditions, poor internal conditions can contribute to persistent coughing, breathing problems and repeated illness.

Where a property also has wider defects, such as leaks, deteriorating plaster, cracked walls, broken windows or unsafe electrics, the risks increase further. These are issues landlords are expected to address promptly, and when they are left unresolved they can create an environment that feels unsafe and unstable, rather than a place where tenants can properly rest and recover.

The consequences can be financial as well as physical. Tenants may face ruined furnishings or clothing, higher energy costs caused by ineffective heating or draughts, and the pressure of trying to manage temporary fixes while waiting for action. Over time, the worry and disruption can impact sleep, concentration and overall wellbeing, leaving people feeling worn down in their own home.

No tenant should be left to cope alone. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim in Barnsley?

No, there are laws in place protecting Barnsley Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent in Barnsley if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments in Barnsley throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
