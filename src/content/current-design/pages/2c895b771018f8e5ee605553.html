Cookies

Cookies are small amounts of information stored by your computer when you log onto our website. Cookies can store information about your preferences and other information which you need when you visit a website. We use cookies to help us improve usage of our site and to keep track of the pages you visit.

Cookies cannot harm your computer and do not contain any personal or private information. If you are happy for us to send cookies to your browser to help us improve user experience, you do not need to do anything. However, if you would prefer to opt out of receiving cookies, you will need to change your browser’s privacy settings – please refer to the help section of your browser for an explanation of how to do this. You should be able to select that your browser rejects all cookies by default or alerts you when each new cookie request is received and you may also be able to delete individual cookies from sites that you have viewed in the past – although please note that this may well affect the performance of the site and your own experience.
