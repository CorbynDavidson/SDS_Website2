Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

If serious disrepair has affected your home in Worthing, you may be able to claim compensation and require your landlord to carry out the repairs needed. A housing disrepair claim recognises that you have been paying rent for a property that has not been kept in the condition your landlord is legally required to maintain.

The value of a Worthing Housing Disrepair Claim will depend on the facts of your case. This includes how serious the disrepair is, how long the problem has continued, when your landlord was first notified, and how much of your home has been affected. If essential rooms, services, or facilities have been difficult to use, this may also increase the level of compensation available.

Compensation is often assessed by looking at the rent paid during the period of disrepair and applying a percentage based on the severity of the problem. As a general guide, many claims fall between 25% and 50% of the rent paid for the affected period. Where the disrepair is particularly severe or long-running, the amount may be higher.

The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Worthing Residents

Living with disrepair can make your home feel uncomfortable, unsafe, and difficult to enjoy. For tenants in Worthing, damp and mould can become a particular concern where there are leaks, poor insulation, inadequate ventilation, or heating problems. These conditions may irritate the lungs, worsen asthma, trigger allergies, and create added risks for children, older tenants, and anyone with existing health concerns.

Other defects can place your safety at risk. Leaking roofs, cracked walls, damaged windows, unsafe electrics, defective flooring, or broken heating systems can interfere with everyday life and make parts of your property hard to use. When these problems are reported but not properly repaired, tenants can be left living in conditions that no one should have to tolerate.

The effects can also be costly. Damp, leaks, and mould may damage clothing, furniture, carpets, and personal possessions. Poor heating or draughty rooms can lead to higher energy bills, while some tenants feel forced to pay for temporary measures just to make the property manageable.

The emotional strain is often just as serious. Repeatedly chasing repairs, worrying about your family’s health, and living in a home that feels neglected can cause stress, poor sleep, and frustration. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Worthing Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a Worthing disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
