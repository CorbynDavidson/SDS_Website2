How We Manage Your Claim

Comprehensive Case Assessment

Every housing disrepair claim begins with a thorough evaluation of your situation. Our solicitors will:

  • Listen to your concerns and understand the full extent of the disrepair.
  • Review the evidence, such as photographs, videos, and correspondence with your landlord.
  • Assess the legal merits of your claim, including the landlord’s obligations under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 and other relevant legislation.

We provide honest advice about your claim’s prospects and outline the potential outcomes before you commit to proceeding.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to resolve a housing disrepair claim?

The timeline depends on the complexity of your case and your landlord’s response. Straightforward claims may be resolved in a few months, while more complex disputes could take longer.

Can I claim if my landlord has started repairs?

Yes. If your landlord delayed addressing the issue or the repairs are incomplete, you may still be entitled to compensation.

What happens if my landlord refuses to cooperate?

If your landlord fails to respond or resolve the disrepair, we can escalate your case to court. Our team will guide you through this process and handle all legal proceedings on your behalf.
