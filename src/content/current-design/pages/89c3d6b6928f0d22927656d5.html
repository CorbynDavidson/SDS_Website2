Understanding Housing Disrepair in Kettering

Housing disrepair refers to a failure by landlords, whether North Northamptonshire Council or housing association, to keep a rented property in good repair. This includes maintaining the structure and systems essential for a habitable home, such as heating, plumbing, and electrical safety. Common disrepair issues tenants face include:

Compensation for Housing Disrepair Claims

Compensation for housing disrepair depends on the seriousness of the problem, how long it has continued, and how it has affected your daily life. If essential parts of your home such as your kitchen or bathroom have not been usable, you may be entitled to a percentage of your rent for the time those areas were out of use.

In many cases, tenants can expect to receive between 25% and 50% of their annual rent as compensation. In more severe situations, this amount may be higher. The Impact of Housing Disrepair on Kettering Residents

Living in a home that has fallen into disrepair can affect every aspect of your life, your health, your finances, and your sense of security. In Kettering, where many properties are older and the climate contributes to persistent damp and condensation, issues such as mould are especially common. These problems may seem minor at first, but they can quickly lead to more serious consequences, including asthma, chest infections and long-term respiratory conditions for both adults and children.

Structural defects are another concern for many tenants. Problems like leaking roofs, cracked walls, faulty electrics, or broken heating systems can make homes cold, unsafe and unsuitable to live in. Not only are these conditions uncomfortable, they also breach your landlord’s legal duty to provide you with a safe and habitable property.

The financial effects of disrepair are just as damaging. From replacing belongings ruined by damp to paying higher energy bills due to poor insulation or broken heating, many tenants face extra costs they should not have to bear. On top of this, the emotional impact is often severe. Stress, frustration, embarrassment and even feelings of isolation are commonly reported by tenants living in unfit housing.

You do not have to continue living this way. If your landlord is failing to take action, legal advice can help you demand the repairs you need and pursue compensation for the hardship you have faced.

What authorities govern housing disrepair in Kettering?

Social housing in England can be handled by either a Council, which is a government authority, or by private Housing Associations which rent out properties. In Kettering, and in the wider East Midlands area, your local Council is likely to be North Northamptonshire Council however other authorities may operate in the surrounding areas.

 

  • Kettering Town Council
  • Kettering City Council

 

If your property is under the responsibility of a private Housing Association, this organisation must be properly registered and approved by the UK Government to operate in England. You can check the details of your association on the list of registered providers on the Government’s website. In Kettering and East Midlands, some of the most common housing associations include:

 

  • Martha Wallis Court
  • English Churches Housing Group
  • Servite Houses

 

Other private providers operate in Kettering, and you can still make a claim for disrepair issues if you are dealing with a provider who does not have a local office in Kettering.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get evicted while making a claim?

No, there are laws in place protecting Council and Housing Association tenants from being evicted for starting a housing disrepair compensation claim against their landlord. 

Do I still need to pay rent if I’m making a claim?

Yes, you should continue regular rent payments throughout a disrepair claim unless you have come to an agreement with the landlord about reducing or withholding rent. Rent arrears could make you liable for eviction, even if you have reduced your rent payments by a reasonable amount while the issues is not resolved or during the repairs if these affect the function of the house or some of the rooms.
